[question]
相同的搜索意图，怎么才能有抢到部分用户流量

[try to solve]
For the same intent, the goal is share—not sole #1. Levers by priority:

1. SERP click share (highest current leverage): pages already show impressions with CTR ≈ 0. Put the query early in the title; open the description with the intent plus a differentiator (free to compute / formula visible / local phrasing). Japanese MR already ranks top 10 with zero clicks—winning clicks beats chasing Omni authority first.

2. Landing-page differences (retain visitors and help re-rank): on the same intent, fill gaps Omni pages often leave—instant calculator + visible formula table + numeric example + boundaries/disclaimer + authoritative references; results above the fold, not wall-of-text.

3. Language share: Omni has no zh; localize es/pt/ja/de to real local query phrasing and take same-intent, different-language slices instead of head-on English head terms.

4. Scenario slices on one page: male/female BMI, increase/decrease %, etc.—do not split URLs; cover via Use cases/FAQ to earn long-tail impressions without doorways.

5. Rank climb via time and internal links: cross-link the calculator cluster + IndexNow; not batches of near-duplicate pages.

6. Bing/Copilot: quotable structure (question H2s, tables, early answers) + IndexNow for citation visibility beyond clicks.

Do not: dual URLs for one intent, copy Omni body text, or create a page per near-synonym. Realistic path: win CTR on pages that already impress → deepen information gain on the same page → language slices → then English head-term share.
