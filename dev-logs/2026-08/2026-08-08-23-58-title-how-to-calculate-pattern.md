Do not make the title a bare “How to Calculate {topic}” sentence. Match live finance/health pages: front half covers “{Topic} Calculator”, back half covers “How to Calculate …”.

A how-to-calculate-* slug does not require the English title to mirror that slug; zh/ja etc. use local phrasing such as “compound interest calculator” in the local script, not a hard English “How to calculate” shell.

Compound-interest brief title is currently `Compound Interest Calculator — How to Find Future Value` (already includes calculator). To align with ROI, prefer `Compound Interest Calculator — How to Calculate Future Value` (or How to Calculate Compound Interest).
