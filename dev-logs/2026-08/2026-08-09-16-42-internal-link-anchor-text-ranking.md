[try to solve]
Yes it helps, but as a relevance and understandability signal—not by stuffing keywords for rank manipulation.

Google’s Link best practices: links help judge page relevance and discover crawlable pages; anchor text tells people and Google what the destination is. Internal-link anchors are especially useful for understanding the site and finding other pages. Good anchors are descriptive, reasonably concise, and relevant to both source and target; surrounding context counts. Avoid “click here / learn more”, empty anchors, and keyword stuffing (spam).

For this site: Related / topical internal links should use the target tool’s natural localized title as anchor text; keep crawlable `<a href>` and avoid orphan pages. Do not build an exact-match anchor factory just for rankings.

Authority: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable
