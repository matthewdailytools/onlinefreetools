Yes — no policy violation. Google has no hard SEO/spam “TTFB must be &lt;X” rule. Ranking-related metrics are Core Web Vitals (LCP≤2.5s, INP≤200ms, CLS≤0.1 at CrUX p75); TTFB is diagnostic and only indirectly affects LCP. web.dev rates TTFB ≤0.8s good and &gt;1.8s poor — 333ms is well inside good. Most users/return visits are HIT (~tens of ms), so p75 is usually better. Do not chase this 333ms for SEO compliance alone; warmup/experience work is optional, not a policy requirement.
