## Context
After shipping `batch-convert-web-pages-to-jpg`, compare the SERP-typical converter page that matches Planner head terms (`webpage to jpg`, `webpage to jpg converter`).

## Process
Fetched https://www.freeconvert.com/webpage-to-jpg (title, meta description, canonical, visible H1/H2/body). Contrasted with our English title/H1 `Batch convert web pages to JPG` and long meta description.

## Root cause / analysis
Their page is built for the **single-URL head query**, not the batch task. Structural wins: slug `/webpage-to-jpg` = query; title `{pair} Converter - {brand}`; H1 `{pair}`; snippet-length meta (~155 chars); How numbered to UI labels; Advanced settings documented as H2; webpage→other-format and *→JPG hubs.

Weaknesses we should not copy: 80+ near-identical “X to JPG” lists (scaled/doorway risk); generic “What is a JPG” encyclopedia; leftover PDF copy in JPG settings (“PDF pages are portrait…”); “Best …” superlatives.

## Solution
No product change in this turn. If we later enrich the batch page, absorb (do not split a doorway): tighter SERP meta; H2 that names viewport/delay/print CSS the way their settings block does; keep batch H1 to stay distinct from a single-URL converter.

## Notes / boundaries
Google people-first / scaled-content policy still forbids cloning their hub of empty conversion URLs. Our IG remains viewport/quality/full-vs-first-screen plus neighbor split vs PDF batch.
