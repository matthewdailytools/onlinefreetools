## Context
`05` listed every sound-app job without site or JS filters. This round asked to sort those jobs and give a build rhythm using demand, technical difficulty, and implementation cost.

## Process
Collapsed 05 into capability packs (not per-button). Scored D/T/C on a 1–5 scale, with cost including licensing, GPU/API, and legal—not only engineering. Mapped packs to layers L0–L4 and waves W0–W6. Called out buy-vs-build and a parallel content/legal track. Linked from README, `00-request.md`, and 05 §25.

## Root cause / analysis
High demand is not the same as “do first.” Celebrity covers, song ID, and realtime voice change all score high D but are blocked by law, fingerprint databases, or hard-realtime routing. The editor door and the cheapest real version of each of the six post-editor jumps (fix / identity / split / fill / understand / publish) maximize closed loops before expensive models.

## Solution
New doc `docs/sound-editor/06-demand-difficulty-cost-roadmap.md`. Sequence: workbench → repair + V0 voice + seed library → stems/STT/TTS → self-clone and generative fill → realtime/video → licensed cover and trust tooling. Unauthorized imitation stays L4.

## Notes / boundaries
06 is not a `work-tasks` schedule and does not expand the onlinefreetools editor slug past W0–W1 DSP. Team-size estimates are relative (one wave ≈ 6–10 weeks at 4–8 people).
