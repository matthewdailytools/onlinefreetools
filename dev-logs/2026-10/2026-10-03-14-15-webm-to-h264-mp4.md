## Sixth locally completed item

`/tools/convert-a-webm-file-to-an-mp4-file` reads the WebM container and source video/audio tracks, checks H.264 encoder availability on the device, then explicitly requests AVC/H.264 video transcode and AAC audio when a source audio track exists. A video-only WebM stays video-only. This avoids Mediabunny's default possibility of copying VP9 into an MP4 container without achieving the page's H.264 playback target. The page reports source codecs, dimensions, approximate duration, input size and actual MP4 size, with video preview and download. Quality and AAC mono controls change actual output; a step progress HUD, cancellation and retry are included.

The result is reopened before success and checked for H.264 video, AAC when source audio exists, and matching duration; this prevents a decoder failure from leaving an audio-only MP4 labeled as a video conversion. The code caps source files at 500 MiB with OPFS and 80 MiB without. These are protective limits, not tested maximums. The tool is local only and has not been deployed. The existing MKV→MP4 conversion uses the same generated loader; its sample download was regression-tested after the loader change.
