## Seventh locally completed item

`/tools/convert-a-mov-file-to-an-mp4-file` reads QuickTime MOV video/audio tracks, dimensions, duration and codec support. Compatible H.264 source video is copied into MP4, while existing sound is written as AAC; settings include AAC mono and quality. Non-H.264 video is only attempted when this browser can decode the source and encode H.264. A HEVC MOV unsupported by the current Chrome is rejected before conversion. The output is reopened and checked for H.264 video, AAC when source audio exists, and plausible duration, preventing an audio-only MP4 from being called a successful video conversion. The result reports source codecs, the video copy/transcode decision, duration and actual input/output bytes, then offers preview and download.

The 500 MiB with OPFS and 80 MiB without OPFS source limits are code caps, not verified maxima. This tool is local only, not deployed. The largest tested input and output here were each about 130 MiB, with a 90-second duration.
