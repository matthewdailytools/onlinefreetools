## Ninth locally completed item

`/tools/batch-convert-mov-files-to-mp4-files` accepts up to 20 local QuickTime MOV clips. It inspects each row, copies compatible H.264 video packets without a video re-encode, converts existing audio such as PCM to AAC, or encodes other decodable video to H.264 when the device supports it. Unsupported HEVC fails only its own row. The resulting MP4 is reopened to verify a real H.264 video track, expected audio and plausible duration, preventing an audio-only false success. Each row reports source and output tracks, video path, dimensions, duration, sizes and errors; completed outputs survive partial failure, stop and retry. Duplicate names get distinct MP4 names.

The batch is processed serially and downloads files one by one. OPFS retains large outputs until removal or Clear; no large ZIP is assembled in tab memory. Without OPFS, each input is capped at 80 MiB and all retained output at 128 MiB; with OPFS the code input cap is 500 MiB. These are code limits, not validated maximum capacities. The real test used a 130 MiB, 90-second MOV and downloaded a >80 MiB MP4 with H.264 video and matching duration.
