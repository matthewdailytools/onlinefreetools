## Sixteenth local item

`/tools/change-video-speed` accepts a local MP4, MOV or WebM, previews it, and exports H.264 MP4 at 0.5–2×. Video timestamps are retimed; users choose pitch-following audio, approximate WSOLA pitch preservation for sources up to 60 seconds, or mute. The result reopens the output to report actual video/audio durations, codec, size and storage path. A large result uses browser-local OPFS. The UI has visible progress, stop and retry. Pitch preservation can introduce artifacts; AAC adds a small tail. No deployment was made.
