About OnlineFreeTools.org

OnlineFreeTools.org is a learning project that ships small, free utilities for mobile and desktop — with formulas and steps shown clearly.

Who

This site is maintained as an iterating learning project. Tools are built for people first: fast, free, and understandable.

How

Pages are rendered on Cloudflare Workers (SSR) so content is crawlable. Calculators show the formula, process, and examples. We do not sell rankings or hide how results are computed.

Why

We publish tools to help people solve concrete tasks — not to mass-produce thin pages for search engines.

Disclaimers

Health and finance tools (such as BMI or ROI) are for education only and are not medical, legal, or investment advice. Verify critical decisions with a qualified professional.
