# Direction

A mixed-container input is the distinct task: one MOV, WebM, MKV or MP4 file, verify source tracks, choose H.264 video packet copy where safe or transcode when needed, encode audio to AAC, and audit output tracks. This differs from pair-specific landing pages because the user may not know the source extension or wants one compatible target. No claim of universal input codecs: unsupported decoding/encoding must fail visibly. Large files use the existing Mediabunny BlobSource + OPFS path.
